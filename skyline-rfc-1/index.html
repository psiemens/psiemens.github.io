Intent

An addition to a small 1962 split-level on a steep site. This project has 3 goals:

  • Create more living and working space for a growing family
  • Preserve the mid-century design language of the existing home
  • Embrace the natural topography and foliage of the site

The proposed addition is a bedroom suite and office, set over the existing garage and a new front entryway. The two upstairs rooms are joined by a bridge hallway that is expressed through a channel glass front.

The aim is for the new floor to read as an extension of the natural slope. A long planted roof runs from the carport back into the hill. The new rooms sit at this elevated grade while the garage, parking and utility space is framed as being below grade. This is an attempt to draw the viewer’s eyes horizontally across the facade, rather than greeting them with a towering two-storey wall upon arrival.

Option A: Bump-out

Bump-out with a wrap-around corner window, under its own low roof. The rest of the 1962 wall line stays.

Option B: Glazed corner nook

The south-west wall carries out to a new west wall, with windows all round and a built-in bench wrapping the corner. Its roof meets the existing one in a valley. Replaces the 1962 west wall, which carries the roof, so a beam goes in its place.
