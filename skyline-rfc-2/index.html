Changes since RFC #1

This is the second round of comments. Please see RFC #1 for an overview of the project.

  • A new stair. The stair now rises from the existing north room, inside the house. The stair that wound up from the mudroom is gone.
  • Second floor re-planned. One large office, a small second office, a lobby and landing, and a primary suite with an ensuite and an open walk-in closet.
  • A new entry and mudroom. With the old stair gone, the front door sits 0.3 m deeper and the channel glass now lines up directly above it. The north wall turns to run parallel to the house, on both floors. The mudroom gets one long run of closets, a guest bench, a family bench and a new window. Question 02 asks for your comments on it.
  • No dining addition. The 1962 west wall stays as it is.

A new stair

The stair now starts in the corner of the existing upper north room and runs straight over the garage to a landing outside the primary suite. It replaces the stair that wound up from the mudroom, so the second floor is reached from inside the house.

Second floor

Re-planned around the new stair. Nobody walks through the bedroom to reach the offices.

  • Office 1 keeps the whole office block. It is a multipurpose room serving as an office, playroom, study, library or even a future bedroom.
  • Office 2 is a smaller auxiliary office for meetings and phone calls.
  • Primary suite is redesigned with the closet and ensuite along the north wall.

Ground floor

The old stair is gone. The front door sits 0.3 m deeper, and the hall from the house opens straight into the entry and mudroom, with no door. Question 02 has the mudroom in detail.

The west side

The dining addition is off the table for now, so the 1962 west wall stays as it is today.

Smaller changes

  • Windows. The six south-facing windows share one sill and head, the office’s south wall is glazed corner to corner, and two frosted clerestories sit on the north wall.
  • Level upstairs. The office floor now matches the rest of the second floor, with no step at its door.
  • The old stair corner is cut off. On both floors, the corner where the old stair began is replaced by one straight wall, square to the living room’s north wall and in line with the carport roof’s west edge, which moves out to meet it. The office and the mudroom each get a window in it.
  • Planted roof inside the setbacks. The carport roof is 1.2 m shorter at its east end, so it stays inside the required front-yard setback.

Windows

The six windows facing south and south-west now share one sill and one head.

Inside the setbacks

In RFC #1 the planted carport roof ran about 1.2 m past what the front-yard setback allows. Eaves may project 0.76 m into a setback. The roof’s east edge now stops at that line, so the roof is 1.19 m shorter at its east end. The storage behind the walkway moved its back wall 1.0 m west, onto the setback line, so it is now about 1.15 m deep inside: still room for bins and yard tools.
